FForgeTrack

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

ForgeTrack ("we", "us") is a fitness app that builds a workout and meal plan for you and tracks your progress. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

DataExamplesWhy
AccountName, email address, password (stored only as a scrambled hash). If you use Google sign-in: your Google name and email.To create your account and log you in.
Health and fitness answersGoal, sex, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level, training experience, injuries you choose to tell us about.To calculate your calories and build your workout plan.
Food preferencesDiet type, allergies and foods you can't eat, foods you dislike, meals per day, cooking, food budget.To build a meal plan you can eat and afford.
Daily logsWeight, waist, calories, protein, water, steps, sleep, workouts, lifts, notes, ticked tasks.To track your days and create your progress reports.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party analytics or ads.

How we use it

  • To build, show and adjust your personal plan, daily tasks and progress reports.
  • The ForgeTrack team may review user answers and logs, individually or combined, to improve plans and add features. We only use it for ForgeTrack.
  • We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) servers in Singapore, which we use as our hosting provider.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google confirms who you are. We receive your name and email from Google, nothing else.
  • Other users can never see your data. Inside ForgeTrack, only you and the ForgeTrack team can.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Security

All traffic uses encrypted HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Every request is checked so that a user can only read and change their own records.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, answers, plan, daily logs and reports straight away. You can also ask us by email; see Account deletion. We do not keep copies after deletion.

Your rights

  • See and download your data: Me → Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit your log, plan or answers in the app.
  • Delete it: Me → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent: deleting your account stops all processing.

Age

ForgeTrack is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Not medical advice

ForgeTrack gives general fitness information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact details are shown in the Google Play listing.
