Delete your ForgeTrack account

You can delete your ForgeTrack account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open ForgeTrack and log in.
  2. Tap Me at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with your password (or type DELETE if you use Google sign-in).

Option 2: ask us by email

Email the address shown in the Google Play listing from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my ForgeTrack account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything: your account (name, email, password hash), your setup answers, your plan, every daily log, and every progress report. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We do not keep any of it afterwards.

Want a copy first? In the app, tap Me → Download my data before deleting.
